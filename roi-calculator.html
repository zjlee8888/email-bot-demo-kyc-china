Appendix

Approach and sources

Approach. Driver-based: each lever builds a gross annual value pool from the stated inputs and benchmarks, less program cost at fixed list price → net annual value, return multiple, payback. Value is stated at gross first-year premium. Scenarios flex the uplift benchmarks only.

  1. Sun Life Hong Kong FY2025 — HK$11.8B new business APE, +46% YoY; broker +144% (No. 1 in market), bancassurance +53%, agency +50%. Press release, April 2026.
  2. Operating benchmarks — Fill Easy deployments and solution brief: NIGO ~50%→10%; ~12% drop-off with ~60% recoverable same-session; ~2 hrs → ~5 min handling per case; ~80% Mainland hospital coverage incl. all major Grade 3; 19 recruit checks, 6–8 weeks → minutes; loaded staff cost ~HK$250/hr (market standard).
  3. Claims fraud — ICAC prosecutions: HK$37.5M critical-illness fraud on bogus medical certificates (jail up to 6 years 4 months); HK$26M syndicate across three insurers. Modeled leakage ~10% with ~50% captured at source.
  4. Pricing — Fill Easy list price, fixed: HK$250 per full onboarding case, HK$150 per bill verification, HK$800 per recruit screen.
