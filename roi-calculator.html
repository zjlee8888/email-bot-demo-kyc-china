How the model works

Three steps, applied to each use case.

1

Size the book

APE1 ÷ average premium = policies per year, scoped to each use case.

2

Cost today's workflow

Price what manual checks lose now: drop-offs, rework, staff hours, leakage2.

3

Net the value

Recovered value − fees = net value, return multiple, payback.

Appendix

Methodology, notes and references

Methodology. Driver-based: gross value pool from the stated assumptions, less program cost at plug-in unit prices → net annual value, return multiple, payback. Premium counted at the value margin, not face premium. Scenarios flex benefit-side drivers only.

  1. Sun Life Hong Kong FY2025 — HK$11.8B new business APE, +46% YoY; broker +144% (No. 1 in market), bancassurance +53%, agency +50%, industry +12%. Press release, April 2026.
  2. Onboarding benchmarks — Fill Easy solution brief (2026): NIGO ~50%→10%; ~12% drop-off, ~60% recoverable same-session; ~2 hrs → ~5 min per case; HK$2,000 per rework case; ~3-day average visitor stay.
  3. Claims benchmarks — solution brief: ~10% leakage on paper bills; ~80% Mainland hospital coverage incl. all major Grade 3; OCR + assessor review eliminated on straight-through claims.
  4. Agency benchmarks — solution brief: 19 checks per recruit; 6–8 weeks of GL23 evidence by hand today; ~15-minute turnaround as one request.
  5. Value margin & unit prices — VNB-style margin, illustrative at 55%; calibrate to the insurer's reported margin. Per-verification fees are placeholders pending quoted pricing.
