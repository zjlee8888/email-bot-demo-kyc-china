Appendix

Sources & method

Method. Each product line computes a gross annual benefit from the assumptions shown, subtracts Fill Easy verification fees at the plug-in unit prices, and reports net value, ROI multiple (net ÷ fees) and payback (months of benefit needed to cover a year of fees). Premium-side benefits are taken at the value-margin haircut, not at face premium. Sensitivity bars show how far each line's net value swings when a single driver moves ±20% from its current setting, holding everything else constant.

  1. HK$11.8B new business APE, +46% YoY; broker +144% (No. 1 in market), bancassurance +53%, agency +50%, industry +12% — Sun Life Hong Kong press release, “Sun Life Hong Kong Achieves Record-Breaking HK$11.8 Billion in Full-Year New Business APE in 2025” (April 2026).
  2. Onboarding benchmarks — Fill Easy solution brief: ~50% NIGO falling to ~10%; ~12% of applications lost to slow underwriting with ~60% recoverable same-session; ~2 hrs agent + back-office effort per case falling to ~5 minutes; HK$2,000 per rework case (illustrative).
  3. Claims benchmarks — Fill Easy solution brief: ~10% leakage on paper bills re-checked by hand; source retrieval across ~80% of Mainland hospitals incl. all major Grade 3; OCR + assessor review eliminated on straight-through claims.
  4. Agency benchmarks — Fill Easy solution brief: 19 checks per recruit; 6–8 weeks of GL23 “fit and proper” evidence gathered by hand today; 15-minute turnaround via one request.
  5. Unit prices — per-verification fees are commercial placeholders. Replace with quoted pricing for a live proposal.
